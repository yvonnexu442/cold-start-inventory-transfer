# Generated Outputs

The public evidence record for the submitted experiments is under
`ai_darld_v3/`.

Included outputs are aggregate summaries, manifests, redacted product-level
figure inputs, frozen role manifests, and small walkthrough artifacts. Raw
datasets, local checkpoints, and full row-level evaluation outputs are not
redistributed. UCI and Favorita role manifests retain source catalog item keys
needed to reconstruct the published splits.

Use `docs/REPRODUCIBILITY.md` for the mapping from result families to scripts,
configurations, and interpretation notes.

The main service-parts summaries are generated from the unredistributed
row-level authority parquet. A full raw-data rerun creates that parquet through
the documented policy, global-baseline, validation, and finalization commands;
the public aggregate CSVs allow result inspection without the raw datasets.
